Booking flow, from a client's side

Walked end to end on desktop and phone, using your real availability and settings.

It's good. Clean, calm, fast, no clutter. On a phone nothing overflows and nothing is cramped. A client who knows who you are can book in about twenty seconds.

Seven things I'd change. Two are small bugs, three are about money and clarity, two are judgement calls for you.

Times don't say Eastern where it matters Bug

I added the timezone label to the summary rows, but not to the time buttons — which is exactly where an out-of-town client is choosing. Someone in Chicago picks "1:00 PM" believing it's their 1:00.
