Money

These can charge the wrong amount, the wrong card, or nothing at all. Do these first.

01

Clients can't replace a saved card

The portal offers it, but the browser never receives the Stripe customer reference, so the request fails. A client with an expired card has to come to you.

02

Every booking creates a new Stripe customer

One person ends up with many customer records, each holding a different card. Different sessions can charge different cards, and "use this card going forward" can't work until this is fixed. 8 of your 14 clients have a card on file, so this is live.

03

Long sessions get undercharged

You're in minimum billing mode. A 90-minute session booked as 60 still defaults to a 60-minute charge, even though clients are told longer runs bill actual time.

04

Coupons and comps aren't enforced at charge time

Discounts affect the suggested figure in Manage only. Editing the hours recalculates at full price and drops the coupon. A comped session still shows normal charge buttons — one wrong click overcharges a free session.

05

A coupon can vanish silently during booking

If the code is used up or removed between typing and saving, the booking goes through at full price without telling the client. They think they got the discount; you bill them in full.

06

The browser picks which Stripe customer to use

The server trusts a customer ID sent from the page instead of deriving it. Needs the same fix as #02 — resolve it server-side.

Privacy & files

Assessment reports and session audio are the most sensitive things the app holds.

07

Working Genius PDFs and session audio sit on public links

Anyone with the URL can open them, with no sign-in and no expiry. 5 clients have a PDF stored. Needs a private Blob store, which has to be created before I can change the code.

08

Clients are never told sessions can be recorded

No privacy notice, no consent step, no retention statement anywhere in the booking flow. This is the one with legal exposure, not just technical.

09

Recording audio is never deleted, and the end can be missing

Uploads aren't awaited — the page waits a flat 1.5 seconds and finalizes, so the last chunk can be lost. Nothing cleans up the audio afterward.

10

fix.html can destroy a client's only PDF

If the move to storage fails, it clears the original anyway. One bad moment and the report is gone.

Data integrity

Quiet failures. Nothing looks wrong until something's missing.

11

A client booking while Manage is open can wipe your edits

Almost everything lives in one record that's read, changed and written back whole. Two writes at once and the later one silently erases the earlier. This is the one that worries me most in this group.

12

Manage can save anything, unchecked

The server accepts whatever JSON it's handed as your complete business record. A stale tab or a bad request can blank required fields.

13

Dates, phones and coupon values are weakly validated

Discount percentages can exceed 100 or go negative through a direct save.

14

Record IDs are short and random

Unlikely to collide at your scale, but two records could end up ambiguous. Cheap to fix.

15

No backup or restore

All of it is one mutable record. There's no export, no snapshot, no way back if something corrupts it.

Access & abuse

16

Client portal login is email + phone number

Both are findable. Anyone holding them can see a client's history and cancel their session. An emailed one-time code would close this.

17

Portal sessions last 60 days and survive logout

Logging out clears the browser's copy only; the server token stays valid. The resume link also carries the token in the URL.

18

A stranger can lock you out of Manage

Six wrong passwords triggers one global lockout. Repeat every 15 minutes and you can't get in.

19

Most public endpoints still have no rate limit

Only check-client is limited so far. Booking attempts, Stripe setup and calendar lookups can all be hammered.

20

No size limits on uploads or form fields

A PDF is trusted because it claims to be one. No check that the contents are actually a PDF.

Polish

21

Failures after booking are invisible to the client

Calendar, PDF or email can fail while the page still says confirmation is on its way.

22

Times are never labelled Eastern

A client in another timezone can misread their appointment. Confirmation also has no add-to-calendar or portal link.

23

Booking can't be done by keyboard

Days, slots and format buttons are plain divs — no focus, no screen-reader state. Someone using a keyboard can't book.

24

The page is titled just "Heshy"

No description, no favicon, no link preview. Tabs and shared links look unfinished.

25

Cancelling in Google Calendar doesn't cancel in Manage

You hit this one in August and said leave it. Still true.

26

No Stripe webhooks

Refunds, disputes and later payment changes never make it back into the booking record.

27

No dependency lockfile, README out of date

Housekeeping. Installs aren't reproducible and security auditing can't run.
