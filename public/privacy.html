Your privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Sessions may be recorded. Heshy records some sessions so he can concentrate on the conversation instead of taking notes, and so you get a written recap afterwards.

He will tell you at the start of a session if he intends to record it, and you can say no. Saying no doesn't change anything else about the session.

What is collected

  • Your details — name, email address and phone number, so sessions can be arranged and confirmed.
  • What you tell him before the session — your answers to the short questions on the booking form.
  • Your strengths assessment, if you take it.
  • Session notes and recaps, written during or after a session.
  • Audio and a transcript, where a session was recorded.
  • Payment details — your card is held by Stripe, the payment company. Heshy never sees or stores your card number.

How recordings are handled

Where a session is recorded, the audio is transcribed so a summary can be written. Only Heshy has access to the recordings, the transcripts and the notes. They are not shared with anyone else, and they are never sold or used for advertising.

If you would rather a particular session wasn't recorded, say so — at the time, or beforehand by email.

Who else is involved

A small number of services make the booking work. Each sees only what it needs:

  • Stripe — payments and your saved card.
  • Google Calendar and Google Meet — scheduling and video calls.
  • Vercel — hosting for this site and its data.
  • Resend — sending confirmation and reminder emails.

Your information isn't sold, rented, or given to anyone for marketing.

How long it is kept

Your details and session history are kept while you are a client and for a reasonable period afterwards, so returning clients don't have to start again and so records exist for tax and accounting purposes. Recordings and transcripts are kept only as long as they are useful for your work together.

What you can ask for

At any time you can ask to:

  • see what is held about you;
  • have something corrected;
  • have a recording or transcript deleted;
  • have your information removed altogether.

Email heshy@catalystconsultingnyc.com and he'll take care of it.

Your own sessions

You can see your upcoming and past sessions, your payment history and your session recaps at any time in your portal.
