Terms

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version. Time is billed by the hour. Your card is charged after a session and payment is due on receipt. Either of you can stop at any point, with nothing owed beyond the time already spent.

Who you are working with

Sessions are provided by Heshy Perlstein through Catalyst Consulting Partners LLC, in Staten Island, New York. Booking a session means these terms apply to your work together.

Booking and changing a session

  • Sessions are booked for a set length, at a time offered on the booking page.
  • You can move or cancel a session yourself in your portal up to a few hours before it starts. The exact cut-off is shown there.
  • Inside that window, email Heshy and he will sort it out.
  • A session you don't attend and didn't cancel beforehand is billed for the time reserved.

What is billed

  • Hourly, at the rate agreed with you before you start.
  • The booked length is the minimum. A session that runs over is billed for the time it actually ran.
  • Travel time is billed where Heshy travels to you.
  • Work between sessions — reading, calls, preparation — is billed at the same rate, and only where you have asked for it.

You will always be told the amount before it is charged, and you get a receipt afterwards.

Paying

You save a card when you book. It is held by Stripe, the payment company — Heshy never sees or stores your card number.

After a session, the amount is charged to that card, or you are sent a link to pay it yourself. Payment is due on receipt.

If an invoice goes unpaid, work stops until it is settled. That isn't a penalty; it is simply how the engagement is run.

Ending it

The engagement is at will. Either of you can end it at any time, for any reason, without notice. What's owed is the time already spent, and nothing further.

Confidentiality

What you discuss stays between you. Heshy does not disclose your situation, your business or your identity to anyone, and does not use your name as a reference without asking you first.

Some sessions are recorded so he can concentrate on the conversation rather than take notes. He will tell you beforehand and you can say no. How your information is handled covers this in full.

What this is and isn't

Heshy helps you think clearly and decide. He is not a lawyer, an accountant, a licensed financial adviser or a therapist, and nothing in a session is legal, tax, investment or medical advice. Decisions remain yours, and so do their outcomes. Where a matter needs a professional of that kind, he will say so.
